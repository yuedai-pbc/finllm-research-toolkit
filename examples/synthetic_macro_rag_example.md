# Synthetic Macro RAG Example

This example uses synthetic text only. It illustrates the structure of an evidence-grounded macro research workflow without redistributing copyrighted or restricted material.

## Synthetic Source A

The central bank states that recent inflation pressure has moderated, while domestic demand remains uneven. The report emphasizes continued monitoring of credit conditions and external uncertainty.

## Synthetic Source B

The statistical release shows that industrial production improved month over month, while retail sales growth remained moderate. Service-sector activity showed signs of gradual recovery.

## Example Research Question

What are the main macroeconomic signals in the latest public materials?

## Example Output Structure

- Inflation: pressure appears to be moderating based on Source A.
- Demand: domestic demand remains uneven, with moderate retail sales growth.
- Production: industrial production improved month over month.
- Policy implication: credit conditions and external uncertainty remain key monitoring areas.

## Quality-Control Notes

- Every claim should map back to retrieved source text.
- Avoid adding numerical estimates unless present in the source.
- Separate observed facts from interpretation.
